The problem

A brand lives or dies at the employee level.

The website gets the rebrand. The ads get the rebrand. Then an employee sends an email with the old logo, joins a call with a bedroom behind them, and hands over a card with a dead phone number.

Five touchpoints, zero control

Signatures, backgrounds, covers and cards are made by each employee. Nobody checks them and nobody can update them.

Plastic that expires

A new title, a new number, a new logo. Each change means another print run and a box of cards in the bin.

Leads that never reach the CRM

Your team meets 300 people at an event. A month later the contacts are still in phones and jacket pockets.

FAQ

Questions HR and IT ask first.

Does the person receiving my card need an app?

No. Tapping the NFC card or scanning the QR opens a web page in their browser. They save the contact from there.

What happens when an employee changes title or phone number?

HR or the employee updates the profile. The contact card, wallet pass, signature and downloads update from the same record. The printed card keeps working because it only stores the link.

What happens when someone leaves the company?

The admin deactivates the profile, or the directory does it automatically. The card stops showing contact details, the seat is free for the next hire, and the contacts they captured stay with the company.

How does Add to Wallet work?

The employee taps "Add to Apple Wallet" or "Add to Google Wallet" on their card. The pass holds their name, title and QR code, and updates when the profile changes.

Can employees change the design?

No. They choose between three approved signature layouts and edit their own details. Logo, colors and templates belong to the admin.

How do campaigns reach employees?

The admin pushes a campaign from the dashboard. It appears in each employee's campaign feed with the creative, the caption and Share buttons. Sharing is always the employee's choice.

What can HR publish to LinkedIn?

New hire welcomes, vacancies, promotions, work anniversaries, company news and events. Posts go to the company page. HR can publish now, schedule, or save a draft.

Which CRMs are supported?

Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Zoho CRM and Pipedrive. Other tools connect through Zapier, webhooks or the API.

Do you support single sign-on?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan. SAML 2.0 with Microsoft Entra ID, Okta or Active Directory, plus SCIM for provisioning.

Is the product available in Arabic?

Yes. The website, the admin, the employee portal and the contact card work in Arabic and English. LinkedIn posts can be written in either language.

Can the "Powered by ReachN" mark be removed?

It is included on all plans. It is small, sits at the edge of each asset, and never replaces your logo.

Which email clients do signatures work in?

Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail. The employee copies the signature, or the admin deploys it to every mailbox.
